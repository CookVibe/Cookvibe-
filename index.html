How creators make money

CookVibe is built so creators can earn more as their content gets more interaction.

✅ Earnings are interaction-driven

  • More engagement → higher payouts
  • Views, saves, shares, comments matter
  • Consistency (weekly posting) helps

✅ Multiple future revenue streams

  • Subscriptions (platform-level)
  • Tips (especially during lives)
  • Brand + product partnerships

✅ Founding advantage

  • Early creators get higher visibility
  • Badge + recognition baked in
  • Help shape the payout model

✅ Global by design

  • International creators welcome
  • Culture + food stories win here
  • Stage 2 adds translation + subtitles

Stage 1 is a demo (no real payouts yet). This is about validating creator tools, uploads, and workflow so Stage 2/3 can launch clean.
