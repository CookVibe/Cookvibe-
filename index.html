How creators make money

The goal is simple: the more engagement you earn, the more you earn.

  • Subscriptions (platform-wide): creators earn from engagement-based payouts.
  • Tips: viewers can tip during lives or after videos (tips-only, no paywalled lives).
  • Brand/Shop (later): affiliate links, product drops, and creator promos.
  • Leaderboards + awards: badges and prizes as we scale globally.

Stage 1 is testing workflows. Stage 2 adds accounts, real payouts, moderation, and translation/subtitles.
